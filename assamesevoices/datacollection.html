Before you start

A few recording tips

  • Find a quiet place with as little background noise as possible.
  • Hold your phone naturally, as you normally would while using or typing on it.
  • Speak naturally, the way you normally would speak with family at home.
  • Say only the word shown on the screen. The illustration is there to help if the word is unfamiliar.
  • You can listen to your recording and re-record it if needed.
  • Please try to complete all the recordings in one sitting.
